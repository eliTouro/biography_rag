All of these texts are public domain and were obtained from Project Gutenberg. The Project Gutenberg header and footer are kept in each file; the app strips them on upload, which leaves 493,618 words, about 1,794 pages, in 1,725 chunks.

Notes:
- Downloads came from the official Project Gutenberg mirror at aleph.pglaf.org (same `cache/epub/<ID>/pg<ID>.txt` files), because gutenberg.org was returning HTTP 503 "Temporarily Down for Maintenance" during the download.
- "The Many-Sided Franklin" (Ford) and "Benjamin Franklin, Self-Revealed" (Bruce, vols 1 and 2) do not appear in the Gutenberg catalog (pg_catalog.csv), so they were not included.
- Page estimates use words / 275 and are rounded per file. Word counts are whitespace-delimited tokens over the full file, including the Gutenberg boilerplate.
- Year column is the original publication year for works where it is known. "n.d." means not confirmed. Please verify before citing.
