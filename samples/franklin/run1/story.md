## Birth and Family

## Work, Travel, and Reputation

Franklin was fond of reading and sometimes sat up nearly all night to read books, borrowing them through apprentices and returning them early in the morning before they were missed. [D03-C0035] He later spent time in London, traveled to Holland, Germany, and France, and then set out for Havre on his return to America. [D06-C0030][D06-C0098][D07-C0032] He also took a dangerous voyage to France on the sloop Reprisal, where English cruisers chased the small sloop several times, and if he had been caught he could have faced imprisonment or a trial for high treason.
